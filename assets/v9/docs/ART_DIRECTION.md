# V9 scene art integration

Three original transparent atlases were generated for this project, then integrated into the existing renderer. No third-party reference pixels, downloaded fonts, music or images were used.

- facilities.png: four columns/two rows; nursery, recovery, military, mutation, storage, prey processing, royal cradle, entrance. Equipment has no painted room floor.
- objects.png: four columns/three rows; seeds, berries, dead beetle, mushrooms, resin, ore, log, fern, stone, roots, wetland, stump.
- ant-bodies.png: five columns/two rows; black-gold player, crystal, swamp, bone, fungus. First row workers, second queens. Right-facing legless torsos; legs are animated separately from actual movement.

Generation direction: mature natural black-gold chitin, amber lighting, crimson details; transparent backgrounds, independent atlas cells, no text, no existing IP. Facilities were requested as modular equipment rather than whole room illustrations; object silhouettes as near-top-down grounded forest resources; ant bodies with distinct head, thorax, narrow petiole and gaster, with enlarged reproductive queen abdomens.

Terrain geometry follows actual open/seen cells and connections. Construction uses actual excavation work and room progress. Atlas failure retains procedural safe rendering. Existing menu and intro are unchanged.

Fourth atlas: player-castes.png (3 x 2), worker, soldier, heavy, giant jaw, acid, future flyer torso. Distinct anatomy, natural black-gold chitin, right-facing, no baked legs or wings. Flyer is art/data preparation only; no combat unit was unlocked.
